MED HANDOFF CARD

Privacy, plainly.

Med Handoff Card stores your medications, dose states, notes, and optional license token in this browser’s local storage. The app does not run analytics, create an account, or send your health information to us.

Printing, QR sharing, exporting, importing, and buying Plus are actions you choose. QR data is not encrypted; encrypted export uses your passphrase locally and we cannot recover it. Clear your browser data to remove local records.
